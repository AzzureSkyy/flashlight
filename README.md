# Flashlight

Flashlight for Garmin Fenix 8 Solar 51mm by **AzzureSkyy**. Drives the watch's built-in LED (white low/medium/high, red); falls back to a screen light on devices without an LED.

Four curved bars run down the left edge of the screen: HIGH, MED, LOW (white) and RED. The current setting is shown on the right.

- Slide a finger over the white bars: switch between LOW, MED and HIGH
- Tap a white bar: switch to that level (tap the lit bar to turn the light off)
- Tap RED: red light on (tap again to turn it off)
- UP/DOWN: move the highlight between bars
- START: apply the highlighted bar (on the lit bar it turns the light off)
- BACK: turn off and exit

Install: copy `build/Flashlight.prg` to `GARMIN\Apps` on the watch.
